How We Chose: Buying Guide

Choose case versus separate board by routine. Detachable folio and cantilever cases stay attached for lap typing in tiered lecture halls and add trackpads for selecting text and multitasking. Separate Bluetooth boards like the OMOTON are cheaper, work across iPad, phone and laptop, and keep tablet weight down, but need a stand and offer no trackpad.

Trackpads pay off when the iPad is your main computer, speeding essay edits, Split View slides-plus-notes and reference-manager clicks. Backlit keys with iOS shortcuts matter for dim libraries, and full-size spacing beats cramped mini layouts for touch-typists. Battery-free Smart Connector boards never need charging, while Bluetooth boards need monthly top-ups.

Fit is size-specific: check Settings, General, About for your exact iPad generation and buy that listing, since 10.2, 10.9 and 11 inch shells do not interchange. Confirm pencil storage if you annotate PDFs, test hinge rigidity for lap use, and learn Cmd-Tab and Cmd-Space shortcuts to double lecture speed. Learn Cmd-Tab multitasking and record audio backup so fast lecturers never leave gaps in notes.
