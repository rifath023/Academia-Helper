    How to Summarise Research Papers Using AI – Student Guide 2025

    Learn how to summarise research papers using AI tools like ChatGPT, Elicit, and SciSpace. Includes step-by-step prompts, best tools, and critical limitations to know.

    AI toolssummarise research papersliterature reviewChatGPTElicitSciSpace

    Reading and summarising dozens of research papers for a literature review is one of the most time-intensive parts of academic work. AI tools now offer the ability to accelerate this process significantly — but using them well requires strategy, critical judgement, and an awareness of their limitations.

    This guide explains how to summarise research papers using AI tools effectively, ethically, and accurately — so you save time without compromising the quality or integrity of your work. For expert literature review support, Academia Helper is here to help.

    Quick Summary: AI tools like ChatGPT, Semantic Scholar, and Elicit can help you summarise research papers quickly, but you must always verify AI summaries against the original paper. Use AI for initial screening, not as a replacement for reading key sources.

    Why Use AI to Summarise Research Papers?

    When building a literature review, you may need to screen 50–100 papers to find the 20–30 most relevant ones for deep reading. AI tools can dramatically accelerate the screening phase by providing quick summaries that help you decide which papers deserve full attention.

    ✅ Good Uses of AI Summarisation

    • Screening papers for relevance before reading
    • Getting a quick overview of a paper's methodology
    • Identifying key findings and conclusions fast
    • Understanding terminology in unfamiliar fields
    • Organising and comparing multiple sources

    ❌ Poor Uses of AI Summarisation

    • Replacing full reading of core sources
    • Submitting AI summaries as your own work
    • Citing papers you have not verified directly
    • Trusting AI to catch all nuance and limitations
    • Using summaries to generate fake citations

    Best AI Tools for Summarising Research Papers

    🔭

    Semantic Scholar

    Free. Uses AI to highlight the most important sentences in a paper (TLDR feature). Excellent for rapid screening. Works directly on journal articles without requiring you to upload anything.

    ⚗️

    Elicit

    Free. Ask a research question and Elicit finds and summarises relevant papers automatically. Shows key findings, sample size, and methodology at a glance. Ideal for literature search and screening.

    🤖

    ChatGPT (with PDF)

    Upload a PDF to ChatGPT and ask it to summarise the abstract, methodology, findings, and limitations. Works well for individual paper analysis with specific prompts.

    📊

    Consensus

    Free AI-powered research tool. Ask a question and get an aggregated answer based on peer-reviewed papers, showing what the evidence says across multiple studies.

    🔬

    SciSpace (formerly Typeset)

    Upload a PDF or paste a DOI. Ask questions about the paper's content and receive plain-English explanations of complex sections. Especially useful for interdisciplinary research.

    📝

    NotebookLM (Google)

    Upload multiple PDFs and ask questions across all of them at once. Excellent for comparing findings across several papers simultaneously during a literature review.

    How to Summarise a Paper Using ChatGPT – Step by Step

    1

    Upload the paper PDF

    Use ChatGPT's document upload feature (available in ChatGPT Plus). Upload the paper as a PDF file.

    2

    Ask for a structured summary

    Use this prompt: "Summarise this paper in the following structure: (1) Research question or objective, (2) Methodology and sample, (3) Key findings, (4) Conclusions and implications, (5) Limitations acknowledged by the authors."

    3

    Ask critical follow-up questions

    Ask: "What are the methodological weaknesses of this study?" or "How does this paper's conclusion compare to the mainstream view on this topic?"

    4

    Verify against the original

    Always check the AI summary against the actual paper, particularly for key findings, statistics, and quotes. AI can misread figures or subtly misrepresent nuanced arguments.

    5

    Take your own notes

    Write up the summary in your own words and note how it connects to your research question. Do not use the AI output as-is in your literature review.

    Best Prompts for Paper Summarisation

    Full summary promptSummarise this research paper covering: research question, methodology, key findings, conclusions, and limitations. Keep each section to 2–3 sentences.
    Critical analysis promptWhat are the main methodological strengths and weaknesses of this study? Are the conclusions well-supported by the evidence presented?
    Relevance check promptMy research question is [your question]. Is this paper directly relevant? What specific findings or arguments would be most useful for my literature review?
    Compare papers promptI have two papers on [topic]. Compare their methodologies, findings, and conclusions. Where do they agree and disagree?

    AI Summarisation Limitations to Know

    • AI can hallucinate statistics, author names, or findings that are not in the paper
    • Subtle arguments and nuanced conclusions are often oversimplified
    • AI cannot reliably evaluate the quality of a paper's methodology
    • Papers behind paywalls cannot be summarised from URLs — you must upload the PDF
    • Some tools have token limits and may not process very long papers fully

    Final Thoughts

    AI summarisation tools are genuinely useful for accelerating the screening phase of a literature review — but they are not a substitute for reading your most important sources carefully.
