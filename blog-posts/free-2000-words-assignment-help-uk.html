Free Assignment Help: Check Offers & Use Study Resources

By Academia Helper · Updated 8 October 2026 · 4 min read

Check the terms behind free assignment-help offers and find practical study resources without relying on unsupported guarantees.

A “free 2,000 words” advertisement is not enough to establish what you will receive or whether the support is permitted for your assessment. Confirm the current offer, eligibility, payment conditions and intended use in writing. This page does not verify that a promotional writing offer is currently available.

Check the actual terms

QuestionWhat to confirm
What is free?The exact activity, quantity and exclusions, rather than a headline alone.
What becomes payable?Total price, payment timing and any conditions that create later charges.
Is there an expiry?A current date and eligibility criteria, rather than an undated “limited time” claim.
What if something goes wrong?Written cancellation, complaint and revision arrangements.
What is permitted?Your institution’s rules for the specific assessment.

Use free resources to develop your own work

You can use the guides and tools on this site without buying a writing service. Start by decoding the assignment brief. Then use the word-count planner to create a provisional structure. Its allocation is a planning starting point; your rubric determines how much space each section deserves.

For research writing, the annotated literature-review example demonstrates synthesis with an explicitly fictional evidence set. Replace that example with verified sources you have read. Do not turn teaching examples into invented citations or results.

Originality is more than a score

A low similarity percentage does not establish proper attribution, authorship or permission to submit a text. An AI-detector result is also not a guarantee of acceptability. Keep notes about your reading and drafting, cite borrowed ideas and check the assessment’s AI policy.

Before sending documents

Start an enquiry with a short description of your question. Avoid sending university logins, participant data or confidential research materials. Read the privacy notice and ask how files will be handled before sharing them.

Questions to ask your university

  • May someone give feedback on my draft, and at what level?
  • Are proofreading or language-editing services permitted?
  • Must I acknowledge outside assistance?
  • What extension or study-support options are available?

Policies differ. For example, Leeds guidance on revising and proofreading explains its own expectations. Use your university’s current policy rather than assuming a provider’s offer overrides it.
