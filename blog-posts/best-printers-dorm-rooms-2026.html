How We Chose: Buying Guide

Match printer type to volume: light mixed printing under 30 pages a month suits cheap inkjet all-in-ones for scan and copy convenience. Heavy black-essay writers should pick the Brother mono laser for 32 ppm duplex output where toner never dries over holidays. Heavy colour users recoup the EcoTank premium within two terms because bottles hold thousands of pages versus cartridges.

Control running costs from day one: use draft mode for readings, duplex everything, enrol in Instant Ink or buy high-yield carts, and send posters and photos to the library print room. Tricolour cartridges waste ink when one colour empties, so separate-tank and bottle systems win for colour. Track pages per term to decide whether next year needs laser.

Plan for dorm networks: many halls isolate devices or require registration, so USB cable always works as backup and WiFi Direct prints without joining hall WiFi. ADFs matter for multi-page readings, auto-duplex saves paper money, and 60-sheet trays need refilling during dissertation weeks. Measure shelf depth because lasers run deeper than inkjets. Track pages per term and send posters to the library print room to keep dorm running costs lowest.
