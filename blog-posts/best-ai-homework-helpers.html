AI Homework Helpers Compared: Photos, Tutoring & Study Help

Compare five homework-help tools by documented features, access and limitations. Learn how to check answers and follow your assessment rules.

Choose by the help you need

For a photographed maths problem, consider Photomath. For guided practice, consider Khanmigo if you are eligible for learner access. For symbolic calculations, explore Wolfram|Alpha. For textbook or video explanations, check Chegg or Numerade. These are different kinds of help: a quick answer, a guided hint and a recorded solution are not interchangeable.

There are no measured accuracy scores, tested rankings or original product screenshots on this page. “Best” depends on your subject, permitted use and the kind of explanation you need.

Photomath: for a maths problem you can photograph

Photomath’s documentation describes scanning maths and displaying solution steps. Its core steps for equations and expressions are free, while Plus adds explanations and animated support. See the official Photomath overview and the product site for current details.

Before using any answer, compare the recognised expression with your worksheet. A missing exponent changes the problem completely. Copy the expression into your notes, work one step independently and check the final value by substitution. A camera feature is useful only if it reads the intended problem.

Khanmigo: for guided questions

Khan Academy describes Khanmigo as a tutor that prompts learners through a task. Its learner page lists a paid individual plan with US residency and billing conditions; under-18 access requires a parent or eligible school arrangement. Free teacher access should not be advertised as universally free student access. Check learner pricing and eligibility.

A guided approach may suit you when you can identify where your reasoning stops. Ask for one hint, attempt the next step and explain your reasoning back. That interaction is more informative than asking the tool to produce a full solution you cannot reproduce. We have not measured whether it improves learning outcomes compared with the other tools here.

Wolfram|Alpha: for checking calculations and assumptions

Wolfram markets computational tools and Pro step-by-step features for students. Consult its student pricing page for the current plan. It is useful to distinguish a computational result from an explanation of why your course uses a particular method.

For example, a result may depend on whether a variable is real, positive or unconstrained. Include those conditions in the question. Check a symbolic answer at a simple numerical value, and state units explicitly for a physics calculation. Do not treat agreement between two tools as a substitute for understanding the method.

Chegg and Numerade: textbook and video explanations

Chegg Study describes textbook solutions and study support. Before paying, check the exact edition and the current subscription terms. An answer to problem 12 in a different edition may address different data even when the chapter title matches.

Numerade’s subscription guidance describes monthly and annual access after a trial. With any video solution, pause before each step and write your own prediction. Then compare the reasoning, not just the final number. This page does not verify individual video correctness or claim a fixed number of free videos per month.

A practical way to check an AI answer

  1. Restate the task. Include all numbers, units, restrictions and what must be found.
  2. Attempt it first. Keep your working so you can identify the point of disagreement.
  3. Ask for a hint or explanation. Avoid handing over an assessed task for completion.
  4. Verify independently. Substitute a solution, estimate an order of magnitude or compare the method with your course notes.
  5. Redo without the tool. If you cannot explain the method later, the answer has not yet become useful knowledge.

For an essay, verify that every cited source exists and supports the claim. For chemistry, check conservation of atoms and charge. For statistics, ask whether the method’s assumptions fit the data. A fluent explanation can still contain a mistake.

Worked check: a solution you can verify yourself

Consider 3(x − 2) = 12. Dividing both sides by 3 gives x − 2 = 4, so x = 6. Substitution gives 3(6 − 2) = 12. If an answer instead says x = 2, substitution exposes the error immediately. This is an original teaching example, not an output observed from any listed product.

The same habit scales to harder problems: verify boundary conditions, test a simple case and inspect whether the result has the right units. When you cannot perform an independent check, take the disputed step to your teacher, tutor or study group.

Is AI homework help allowed at university?

Permission depends on your assessment instructions. Some tasks allow specified assistance with disclosure; others prohibit it. Ask the module team about the exact use you propose, keep any required record and follow disclosure rules. A provider’s “learning” label does not override your institution’s requirements.

Do not upload confidential research data, another person’s work or restricted assessment material without permission. Consult your institution’s approved tools and support channels before using an external service.

Common questions

Which tool is the most accurate?

We cannot establish that from provider documentation. A fair comparison needs the same problems, independent answer keys, recorded versions and visible failures. This page does not assign accuracy scores.

Can I get help from a photo for free?

Photomath documents free core maths solution steps. Coverage and advanced features differ, so check the recognised input and current app conditions. Free access does not guarantee a correct solution.
