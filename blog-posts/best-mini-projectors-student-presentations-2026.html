How We Chose: Buying Guide

Read resolution honestly: native 480p models accept 1080p input but look soft on text, so keep fonts large and dark rooms darker. Native 720p-plus and 1080p laser models render small citations sharply but cost multiples more. For slide rehearsal, brightness and focus ease matter more than cinema contrast.

Brightness decides the room: budget LEDs near 100 to 200 ANSI lumens need blackout or evening rooms, while 300-plus lumens and laser sources survive dimmed seminar lights. Throw distance matters in small flats, so short-throw pocket models suit close walls. Check HDMI, USB and wireless mirroring for laptops and phones, plus a tripod thread for shelf mounting.

Battery frees societies and outdoor nights but halves brightness on eco modes, so mains plus a speaker cable suits assessed presentations. Lamp life near 30,000-plus hours outlasts any degree, and auto-keystone saves setup time. Keep a clicker and offline slide copy for weak venue WiFi. Keep fonts large on budget natives and carry an HDMI cable plus clicker for venue WiFi failures.
