How We Chose: Buying Guide

Read watts before milliamp-hours. Phones top up on any 10 to 20W output, but ultrabooks need 45 to 65W PD like the INIU, Nano and Blade, and 16-inch performance laptops want 100 to 140W like the Baseus and Anker 737 for full-speed charging. Check your charger brick rating and match it; underpowered banks merely slow the drain rather than refilling during use.

Capacity, ports and displays shape library days. 10000mAh refills a phone twice and stays pocketable, 20000 to 24000mAh refills phones plus half a laptop but weighs a pound-plus, and multi-port 2 to 3 output layouts charge laptop plus earbuds simultaneously with smart power split. Percentage displays beat blinking dots for planning the last seminar.

Weight, safety and flights close the call. Pocket minis near half a pound vanish into coat pockets, bricks near 1.4 pounds anchor backpack bases, and sub-100Wh ratings fly cabin-safe while larger bricks need airline approval. Favoured brands with temperature control and trickle modes protect earbuds, and USB-C PD cables rated for 100W-plus are mandatory to unlock headline speeds. Match cable wattage to the bank or speeds halve.
