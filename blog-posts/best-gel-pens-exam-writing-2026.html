How We Chose the Best Gel Pens Exam Writing Needs

Nib size sets the trade-off. 0.7 mm is the all-rounder for speed and legibility. 0.5 mm suits small handwriting and diagrams but needs a light hand; 1.0 mm glides boldly with least pressure yet dries slowest and can bleed on thin paper.

Ink behaviour under pressure. Standard gel takes seconds to dry, so fast writers smudge. Quick-dry formulas like EnerGel cut smudging notably — the pick for left-handers. Fraud-resistant and archival inks also scan and photocopy cleanly for exam scripts.

Grip and fatigue. Cushioned rubber grips and balanced barrels matter across long papers. Retractable designs save caps; refillable bodies like G2 and EnerGel cut waste and long-term cost across a degree.

Colour systems and value. Black multipacks cover exams cheapest per pen; rainbow sets like Sarasa Clip earn their place in revision for colour-coded headings and dates. Mid-range gel at roughly $1 to $2 per pen in multipacks writes far better than the cheapest ballpoints.
