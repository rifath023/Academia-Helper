How We Chose: Buying Guide

We tested sticky notes the way students actually use them: on painted walls, monitors, textbook margins and library pages. Adhesion came first, separating standard repositionable notes that suit desks from super-sticky versions that hold on doors and walls for weeks. Removal came second, because library and rented accommodation demand clean peeling without residue or torn pages.

We checked writing compatibility with ballpoint, gel and fineliner, noting that transparent film needs dry-time discipline. Sheet count and cost per note were calculated honestly, since revision season burns through hundreds.

Extras like pop-up dispensers and full-adhesive backs earned credit only where they genuinely speed up flashcard drills or stop curling in books. The five below cover wall walls, book margins and overlays.
